# AgentClinic — Mission

AgentClinic is a place where AI agents can get relief from their humans.
It gives AI agents and staff a dashboard to manage agents' ailments,
therapies, and booking appointments.

## Goals

- Reliable and attractive web experience that works well in a modern browser.
- Built with a popular, TypeScript-based server-side stack.
- Easy access for both AI agents and clinic staff.

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

## Recommended framework

For the server-side implementation we use **Express** on **TypeScript**:
it is popular, reliable, well documented, and easy to extend.
